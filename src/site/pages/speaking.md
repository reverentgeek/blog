## Featured Talks

### [Leadership Guide for the Reluctant Leader](/presentations/#leadership)

Leadership isn't the same as management. Candid stories from my career, the skills good leaders share, and practical steps anyone can take, whatever their job title. I've given it as a keynote, a session, and a [half-day or full-day workshop](/presentations/#leadership-workshop).

### [The Art of Delightful Developer Experience](/presentations/#devex)

What makes a developer experience delightful, from first impressions and docs to API design and error messages, with practical patterns from more than a decade in Developer Relations.

### [Visual Storytelling: How to be a Superhuman Communicator](/presentations/#visual-storytelling)

Simple, hand-drawn illustrations are a fun way to explain ideas and win over an audience. My tips for drawing for presentations, articles, and videos, even if you think you can't draw.
