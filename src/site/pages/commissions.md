## Questions Folks Ask

### Why not just use AI?

Fair question! An AI image generator is fast and cheap, and sometimes that's all you need. But it draws what you typed, not what you meant. It won't ask what the joke is, notice that the diagram is backward, or keep your mascot looking like the same character from one drawing to the next.

There's also ownership. In the US, images generated by AI without meaningful human authorship can't be registered for copyright (see the [Copyright Office's guidance on AI](https://www.copyright.gov/ai/)). That matters a lot when the art is your logo, your mascot, or your book cover. Original, hand-drawn work doesn't have that problem.

### How much does a custom illustration cost?

It depends on what you need: how many pieces, how detailed, and how you'll use them. [Send me the details](#custom-work), and I'll send you a quote.

### Can you match my brand or an existing style?

Yes! Send me your brand guidelines, colors, or the characters and style you already use, and I'll draw to match.

### Do you draw at events?

Yes! I've drawn live avatars and sketch notes at conferences on three continents as well as for online events. If you're planning an event, booth, or meetup, [let's talk](#custom-work).
